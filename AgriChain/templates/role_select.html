RBKPOS

Three field workflows. Stock receiving to KCC credit offset.

  1. 1. Receive stock and verify CCRC leases

    Inspect agro-company deliveries at the RBK dock and verify 11-month oral tenant leases with explicit non-encumbrance landowner consent.

  2. 2. Dispense inputs with biometric quota lock

    Match farmer Aadhaar thumbprints against verified owned and leased acreage so subsidized urea cannot be hoarded or resold.

  3. 3. Certify QR lots and settle buyer escrow

    Tag tested harvest sacks with QR traceability passports, lock buyer funds in escrow, and clear KCC input debt automatically on delivery.

Common questions

How does AgriChain prevent fertilizer hoarding?

The Biometric POS requires verified Aadhaar status and checks requested bags against verified owned and CCRC-leased acreage before firing the MySQL stock trigger.

Does CCRC tenant registration affect land ownership?

No. Every 11-month CCRC record enforces a strict expiry ceiling, an explicit non-encumbrance clause, and landowner OTP or biometric consent before VAA approval.

How does buyer escrow settle farmer KCC credit?

When the buyer confirms dock delivery, escrow releases, pays off outstanding active KCC balances first, and routes the net proceeds directly to the farmer.

How are tables and QR labels updated?

Portal forms submit asynchronously to Python API handlers backed by MySQL transactions, updating KPIs, inventory tables, and thermal QR stickers in place.
